Pests and diseaserats, barn owls, beetles, Ganoderma

Barn owl occupancy as reported at each visit. The swings reflect inconsistent counting more than real change.

  • GanodermaAbove 5% in tall palms. The last census was Aug 2024 (7% dead). Premature frond snapping and small canopies across P98C, P99D and P01E.
  • Rats3–6% in 2023, no census in 2024, 1–4% since 2025. Spikes in young blocks: PR23B 20% (Q1 2025), PR24B 11% (Apr 2026). Neighbouring Bukit Jin is at 34–65%.
  • BeetlesOryctes in PR23B reached 21% (Aug 2024). Rhino beetle damage is significant in PR25B (2026).
  • BagwormSigns in P01E6–E7 in 2024, about 30 ha in P98C in 2025, and late-larval defoliation in P01E6–E7 in Jan 2026.
  • NurseryCurvularia in every year. Main outbreak Jul 2026, now recovering.
