Sources: PA Report 1/2026 (Planting Advisor En. AREA (PA 1/2026, as of 31 May 2026): Total 1,170.73 ha. Mature oil palm 974.82 ha (83%), vacant Ganoderma-infested land 190.70 ha (16%), unplantable 5.21 ha. No immature or replant area; no replanting in 2026. Fields: P99A 182.26 ha (1999, SPH 118), P04B 310.31 ha (2004, SPH 109), P05C 234.68 ha (2005, SPH 110), P06D 247.57 ha (2006, SPH 128); average SPH 115. All palms over 20 years old. Estate proposed replanting from 2027 (under consideration). Agro 2/2026: planted area cut from 1,165.52 to 974.82 ha by excluding Ganoderma vacant areas; recommends proper area survey; replanting programme for 2027/2028 should be finalised. Agro Table 3 shows SPH of 136/136/136/148 (avg 139) on current area, but says this SPH, which excludes vacant points and dead palms, is inappropriate and must be reviewed. Terrain flat to undulating; average rainfall about 2,000 mm and 135 rain days over 5 years (PA).

LABOUR AND STAFF (PA 1/2026, 31 May 2026): 67 workers (5 estate checkroll, 62 contractor) vs requirement 72 (short 5), ratio 1 worker:15 ha. Harvesters 46 vs 48 required (1:21 ha); drivers/loaders 6/6; weeding 6/10; manuring 4/8; watchmen 3; general 2. 5 recruited and 2 absconded in 4 months. Five contractors (Desmond Agro, Chua Chee Tiong, Zian-Zian Plantation, Malaiqa Bina, Suhanizan Bin Ishak) each 100-300 ha; PA says minimum economic size is 400 ha per contractor and suggests a contractor evaluation. Machinery: 10 mechanical buffaloes (3 Timac, 6 Agrostar, 1 TF) plus 1 mini tractor. PA 8.3: 5-year yield potential about 22,500 mt implies ideal 42 harvesters and 9 machines (2.00 t/day per harvester, 10 t/day per machine). Staff adequate; junior assistant Loh Wen Min moved to audit. Agro 2/2026 says insufficient manuring manpower is delaying the programme and harvesting manpower must be secured for the 2H peak.

CROP (PA 1/2026): Jan-May 2026 FFB 7,078.90 mt = 7.26 t/ha. PA text says this is a 10% deficit against a budget of 8,664.77 mt, but Table 19 gives an estimate of 9,867.00 mt (10.12 t/ha). Field yields Jan-May (actual vs estimate t/ha): P99A 7.06 vs 9.56; P04B 8.09 vs 9.99; P05C 6.40 vs 10.42; P06D 7.19 vs 10.43. May 2026 column: 7.98, 8.54, 9.53, 9.38, total 8.89. Agro 2/2026 (as at June): 8.82 t/ha vs 11.50 same period 2025 (-23.3%); P99A 8.30 vs 10.52 (-21.1%), P04B 9.88 vs 10.79 (-8.43%), P05C 7.88 vs 12.30 (-35.9%), P06D 8.75 vs 12.33 (-29.0%). Agro attributes declines to seasonal trend and the May-June dry spell; expects improvement in 2H. Monthly production (Agro Appendix 3), mt: 2026 Jan 1,496.46, Feb 1,110.66, Mar 1,447.66, Apr 1,668.28, May 1,355.84, Jun 1,515.92 (1H 8,594.82). 1H totals: 2022 10,387.22; 2023 11,533.73; 2024 12,310.15; 2025 10,595.53. Full-year totals: 2022 22,373.29; 2023 21,148.53; 2024 23,581.34; 2025 21,698.93 mt. PA 2.2 says FFB has stayed above 18.00 t/ha a year for 5 years.

HARVESTING (PA 1/2026): interval 11-14 days over 5 months, 10.58 rounds (summary page says 21.89 rounds vs minimum 22.00). Harvester productivity to date 1.28 t/day (table total 1.23) covering 2.22 ha/day, well below the 2.00 t target and 2.50-3.50 t aim with mechanised collection. Field productivity (t/manday, rate RM/t, earnings RM/day): P99A 1.37, 40.00, 54.80; P04B 1.15, 35.00, 40.25; P05C 1.23, 35.00, 43.05; P06D 1.47, 35.00, 51.45. Earnings below minimum wage; estate to verify data. Ripe crop above 90%; occasional overripe in wet low-lying areas. Agro 2/2026 intervals: Jan-Mar 14-16 days, Apr-Jun 10-14 days; cumulative rounds to June 12.81 (106.75% of target: P99A 12.83, P04B 12.88, P05C 12.58, P06D 12.94). Overripe bunches insignificant on random check, though P05C blocks showed delayed harvesting. PA concluding remark: keep intervals under 15 days.

AS 121.90 done (Jan); NK1 Feb/Mar done, April 39.40 of 114.45 mt (34%), May/June nil; ARP 164.50 done; Mix B nil (late delivery); Mix A nil. Workers 27-30 bags/day earning RM62.90-69.00. Agro 2/2026 to June: 547.40 mt of 1,073.45 mt annual (52%): NK1 261.00 of 414.35 (63%), Mix B 0 of 199.00 (0%, only 30 mt received), RP 164.50 (100%), Mix A 0 of 173.70 (0%, none received), AS 121.90 (100%). Store acceptable. 2027 programme (kg/palm): P99A Mix A 2.00 + NK1 2.00 x2; P04B Mix A 2.00 x4 + RP 1.50; P05C C1-C4 NK1 2.00 x2 + RP 1.50 + Mix A 2.00 x2, C5-C6 NK1 2.25 x2 + RP 1.50 + Mix B 2.00 x2; P06D Mix B 2.25 x2 + RP 1.50 + Mix A 2.00 x2. LEAF NUTRIENTS (Agro 2/2026, P04B/P05C/P06D sampled): N normal-high; P marginally low in C4, C5 and D1-D5; K normal except B2 marginally low (0.92%); Mg/Ca mostly acceptable, Ca marginally low in all P05C, Mg marginally low in D4/D5; B sufficient. Mild K deficiency symptoms in C3/C4 (sandy) and D2/D3 (low-lying). Overall status acceptable.

FIELD UPKEEP: PA: one round circle spraying done on 974.82 ha (100% of to-date programme, 1 of 3 rounds); selective spraying 1,784.41 ha vs 1,764.71 ha to-date programme (table still on old 1,165.52 ha). Sprayer output 4.0 ha (circle) and 4.5 ha (selective) per day. Recommend low-volume Inter Pump/CDA and keeping circles to 2 m radius. Agro: two rounds interrow done in all fields; circle 2 rounds in P99A/P04B, 1 round in P05C/P06D; Clidemia not fully killed (poor wetting), tighten premix supervision. Pruning: PA 2 rounds 100% done; Agro notes many under-pruned palms despite 100% progress. Drains: about 150 ha low-lying flood-prone; desilt P99A and P05C (PA); Agro saw no waterlogging. Roads: grading budget 2,500 chains, none done; backhoe 132 of 850 h. Boundaries: spray and repair trenches; no encroachment seen.

PESTS AND GANODERMA: Rat damage below 3% (PA); Agro Q1/Q2 2.25-3.50%, under 10% threshold, no baiting. 153 barn owl boxes, 86 occupied (56%); PA says 48 damaged; Agro shows 97 good, 56 poor and says 117 boxes would be optimal (1 per 10 ha), occupancy against that ratio is 73%. Bagworm: 3%/2% in Jan (P05C/P06D) then 0%. Ganoderma: PA says 17% of palms dead or infested; Agro census shows 2,189 infected and 21,048 fallen palms (P99A 808/3,124; P04B 911/9,894; P05C 277/3,612; P06D 193/4,418). Agro: infection increasing; new census in five categories required; mound to 1 m where infection exceeds 5%; deboling now improved to specification. PA: P05C deboling/mounding budgeted but not started.

RAINFALL: 2022 1,685.0 mm/120 days; 2023 1,850.5/123; 2024 2,212.0/149; 2025 2,025.0/151; 2026 Jan-Jun 694.5 mm/57 days (Jan 13, Mar 30 and Jun 65.5 mm were very low; Agro flags water stress).
