UPKEEP 2025 (PA).
Circle spraying: 669.49 of 1,190.73 ha (56%), behind because sprayers were moved to manuring. Output 3.3 ha/manday (5 is possible) at RM16/ha, earning RM52.80/day, which is below the basic wage. Glyphosate 0.63 L/ha plus Ally 0.02 kg/ha; Ally is not needed. Circle raking is needed in the old plantings.
Selective spraying: 396.91 of 1,190 ha (33%, 1 of 3 rounds). Inter-rows are Nephrolepis dominant with abundant VOPs, so prioritise VOP eradication. Output 3.7 ha/manday (7-8 is possible) at RM17/ha, earning RM62.90/day. Garlon 0.54 L/ha plus Ally; 8 pumps/day suggests about 5 working hours.
Manuring: 23 of 31 rounds (74%). The September to November rounds were not applied (NK1 for P97 A1/A2 and P98 B; MIX B for C1, C2, D, E, F). Wheelbarrow output 27 bags/manday at RM2.30/bag, earning RM62.10/day; consider revising the rate.

The 276.12 ha balance is due by June 2026, with 4 pruners engaged since July 2025. Costs: progressive RM1.20/palm/year; corrective RM2.30/palm.
