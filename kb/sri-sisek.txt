AREA (PA 1/2026, as of March 2026)
Total planted 319.69 ha (790.10 acres), up 4 ha after acquiring field PR26Q in January 2026. Mature 303.47 ha, immature 16.22 ha. Two entities: Sri Sisek Estate (mature 251.75 ha, immature 12.22 ha, subtotal 263.97 ha) and Sri Sisek Estate Small Crop (mature 51.72 ha, immature 4.00 ha, subtotal 55.72 ha). Mature area grew as PR22H (32.52 ha), PR23N (1.95 ha) and PR22I (4.86 ha) matured. Immature: PR23L2 6.31 ha, PR24M 5.91 ha, PR26Q 4.00 ha. Fields are scattered around Kampung Geddes, bordering smallholders, so crop and input security is a concern. PA says 16.60% of area is old fields over 20 years. PA says about 83% of palms are young mature to prime.
Agro 1/2026 palm profile: prime palms aged 9-19 years make up 61% of area; young mature 37.38 ha (11%); old palms about 27 years old in D1 and D3 total 19.67 ha (6%). Agro says immature is 14% (49.60 ha), which conflicts with the 16.22 ha in both area tables; 49.60 equals 16.22 + 1.95 (PR23N) + 31.43 (PR25P). Replanting of P98D1 (11.74 ha) and D3 (7.93 ha), total 19.67 ha (7.45% of the main estate), is tentatively planned for 2027 because of Ganoderma vacancies, but top management has not confirmed it. Palm census was not updated last year because of manpower constraints. Palm density per block (Agro Table 4): P09A1 139, P09A2 138, P09B1 148, P09B2 148, P06C 120, P98D1 123, P11D2 152, D3 138, P15E 131, P16F 115, P18G 136, P01I/P02K/P05L/P05J/P05O 136, P22H 140, P22I 136, P23N 136, PR23L2 136, PR24M 136, PR26Q 128 (513 palms). LABOUR (PA 1/2026, as of March 2026)
18 permanent workers: 14 checkroll and 4 contract. Harvesters 10 (8 checkroll plus 2 contract), split into two gangs of 5 (Bangladeshi and Indonesian). Driver/loader 4, manuring 2, line sweeper 2. The labour table puts the requirement at 13 workers including 10 harvesters (dashboard uses these), a surplus of 5, but PA para 4.4 recommends 20 workers with 14 harvesters as harvesting area grows. Current ratio is 1 worker per 18 ha and 1 harvester per 30 ha. Recommended: 1 worker per 15 ha, and 1 harvester per 24 ha (para 4.2) or 22 ha (GMD item). From April 2026 the estate stopped using contractors and runs on its own checkroll workers. Agro says harvester numbers met the requirement of 10 after new workers arrived.
