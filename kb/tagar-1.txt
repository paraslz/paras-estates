AREA (PA 2/2026)
Total land 2,103.76 ha. Planted 1,851 ha: oil palm mature 1,563 ha, immature 283 ha, durian 5 ha (the PA field table shows 288.0 ha in its immature column, which is 283 ha oil palm plus 5 ha durian; Agro Table 1 totals 1,846 ha oil palm). Also jungle 199.41 ha, labour quarters 30.24 ha and TNB rentice 23.11 ha. Mature area rose from 1,382 to 1,563 ha when PR22K (180 ha) was declared mature in 2026. Blocks: PM01A 2001 IOI 129 ha; PM04B 2004 Chemara 148; PM05C 2005 Chemara 76; PM07D 2007 Chemara 173; PM11E 2011 DxP 140; PM16F 2016 Yangambi 24; PM17G 2017 Yangambi 36; PM19H 2019 Yangambi 193; PM20I 2020 FELDA 220; PM21J 2021 Yangambi 243; PR22K 2022 Yangambi 180; PR23L 2023 Yangambi 176 (1 ha mature, 175 immature); PR24M/PR25M Yangambi 108 ha. There are no replanting plans for 2026 and 2027. Agro: all palms over 25 years were phased out by 2024; young mature share rose from 36% to 45%. The PR25M (ex-PM99) replant ran from Dec 2024 to Dec 2025 with Cheekah Kemayan seedlings. Agro para 2.6 says 134 palms/ha with 900 points unfilled, but Table 10 shows 12,159 of 14,688 programmed points planted (83%), which leaves 2,529 unplanted. No recent palm census.

LABOUR (PA 2/2026)
96 workers (30 checkroll, 66 contract) against 142 required. Harvesters 51 vs 78 required at 1:20 ha. The PA advises 1:18 ha (87 harvesters) because FFB is moved by wheelbarrow with no mechanisation. Para 3.3 says 70 harvesters as at July (1:22), short 17; 17 new harvesters were due in August. Manuring workers 9 vs 15, sprayers 10 vs 20 (recruitment under way), drivers 6 vs 7, watchmen 6 vs 8, security 4 vs 5. Worker ratio 1:16 (ideal 1:12). The main problems are worker turnover caused by low earnings and low productivity.

CROP (PA 2/2026, Jan-Jun 2026)
FFB 8,393.44 t vs budget to date 12,693 t (annual 27,953 t). Same period last year 8,942.13 t; last year's 12-month total 18,688.50 t. Yield 5.37 t/ha vs 8.12 budget (34% below) and 6.47 last year (17% below). Annual budget 17.88 t/ha. CPO 1,573.55 t at 18.75% OER (budget 19.07%); PK 419.69 t at 5.00%. The shortfall is due to the harvester shortage and early-year low crop. Bunch production has recovered, with good fruit set in P16, P19, P20, P21 and P22. Bunch weight June 2025 to June 2026: 2001 22.1 to 25.6 kg; 2004 21.7 to 23.8; 2005 17.9 to 20.6; 2007 20.9 to 21.8; 2011 15.8 to 17.4; 2016 10.7 to 13.1; 2017 6.0 to 12.7; 2019 8.2 to 8.3; 2020 4.2 to 6.2; 2021 2.4 to 4.3. The PA says the jumps in 2001, 2004, 2005 and 2017 need investigating.

HARVESTING (PA 2/2026)
9.4 rounds to June vs 12 budgeted; average interval 18 days; outturn 104%. Wheelbarrows with a cutter-carrier system. Loose fruit collection needs better supervision. Many runts should be poisoned. Rates, output and earnings: PM01A RM54/t, 1.47 t, RM79.38; PM05C RM45, 0.91 t, RM40.95; PM16F RM37, 1.14 t, RM42.18; PM17G RM37, 1.29 t, RM47.73; PM19H RM69/74, 1.39 t, RM95.91/102.86; PM04B RM49, 1.46 t, RM71.54; PM07D RM41, 1.57 t, RM64.37; PM11E RM40, 1.49 t, RM59.60; PM21J RM94, 1.08 t, RM101.52. Basic wage is RM65.38. Harvesters work only 4 to 5 hours.
Agro 1/2026 (Jan): harvest intervals were over 20 days in older plantings; overripe bunches and loose fruit left in P04, P05 and P07; selective harvesting in ravines. 2025 harvester output averaged under 2.0 t/manday (PM01A 1.59, PM04B 1.71, PM05C 1.28, PM07D 1.72, PM11E 1.70, PM16F 1.48, PM17G 1.58). Only 2,170 chains of road were graded in 2025 (33% of budget).

UPKEEP (PA 2/2026)
Circle spraying: 1,963 ha (1.3 rounds), 60% of the 3,295 ha budgeted to June, because of a sprayer shortage. CDA at 4 ha/manday, RM16/ha, earning RM64/day (below the RM65.38 basic). Glyphosate 0.89 L/ha. Sprayers do 6 pumps a day against a potential of 9 and work about 4.5 hours, leaving before 1 pm; Assistants are not supervising. Nephrolepis in circles needs a contact herbicide round. Selective spraying: 1,914 ha (1.2 rounds), 72% of 2,670 ha, mist blower at 3.4 ha/manday, RM18/ha, earning RM61.20, Garlon 0.82 L/ha, 7 pumps against 11. Overgrown VOPs, Asystasia and woodies in young mature areas.
Manuring: 17 of 40 budgeted rounds applied to June because of late NK and kieserite deliveries. The 2025 carry-forward was finished in February. Mini-dumpers on 195 ha; the rest broadcast at the daily rate. Mg deficiency on P22K terraces, where EFB is advised.
Rats: extensive damage; one baiting round in P19 (128.66 ha), P21 (91.90 ha) and P22 (73.52 ha) was not enough. Ganoderma in P01A is not yet significant. A court case on cattle intrusion is set for September.
Roads: 1,008 of 5,469 chains graded with the tractor back-mounted grader at RM8.50/chain (budget RM12.50). Roads are poor and eroded, so a motor grader contractor is advised. 95.12 t of crusher run was bought at RM45/t, unbudgeted. Drains: 331.77 chains of desilting not done (budget RM46/chain).
Pruning: 1,278.54 of 2,764 ha progressive pruning to June. Corrective pruning: 225 ha from 2025 (P01, P05, P20) with 59 ha done; 271 ha for 2026 (2022, 2007 and 2011 plantings) with 72 ha done, due by October; task force of 11. Cost RM0.44 to RM0.92 per palm per year; corrective pruning RM2.50/palm.

Ganoderma insignificant, but no de-boling done.

PA CONCLUSIONS
Acute shortage of harvesters and sprayers; low earnings drive turnover. Sprayer hours and productivity are poor. Continue rat baiting, finish corrective pruning, improve harvester productivity and improve immature field conditions this financial year.
