LADANG GRANCIA, Jasa Warga Sdn Bhd (Prosper group). Fact pack from PA Report 1/2026 (Planting Advisor Mr. Boey Chee Weng, visit 20 February 2026, reviewing Jan–Dec 2025) and Agronomy Report 1/2026 (Agronomist Mr. Chong Kuan Ming, visit 27 February 2026, follow-up to 30 September 2025 visit). Plantation Controller Dr. Chua Chin Kok; Senior Manager En. Zulkarnain Bin Barik; Sr. Assistant-in-Charge (Agro: Estate Manager) Mr. Muruganathan A/L Arumugam; Assistant Manager En. Muhamad Hafiz Bin Shamsudin, who also manages the rubber at Sg. Beranang Estate. The PA report has no general charges, upkeep cost, harvesting cost or cost of production sections, so no RM cost figures are available for this estate.

AREA (PA, Dec 2025; Agro Table 1): total oil palm 205.00 ha. Mature 146.00 ha: P99A 1999 13.00 ha, P13B 2013 21.35 ha, P16C 2016 60.65 ha, P18D 2018 51.00 ha. Immature PR24E (2024 replant, ex-P87 and P00) 59.00 ha. No change in area. There are no plans yet to replant the 1999 planting; Agro says no replanting was approved for 2026 or 2027. Agro age profile: old (over 25 years) P99 6.3%; prime (8–19 years) P13, P16, P18 64.9%; immature PR24 28.8%. The estate title area is not stated.

PALM DENSITY (Agro Table 2): P99A 1,477 palms SPH 114; P13B 2,871 SPH 134; P16C 8,028 SPH 132; P18D 6,854 SPH 134; PR24E 7,751 SPH 131; total 26,981, average SPH 132. Low density in P99 is from Ganoderma losses.

LABOUR (PA, Dec 2025): total 15 against 16 required. Harvesters/pruners 8 of 8 (1:18 ha). Loaders/drivers 2 of 2. Manurers 1 of 2: Sg. Beranang workers cover when rounds are due. Sprayers 2 of 2; mandore 1; others 1. Ratio is 1 worker to 10 ha. Staff are adequate with no changes.

WEEDING (PA, 2025): mature circle spraying 425 ha (3 rounds), 100%, using Inter Pump low volume at 4.2 ha/man-day, RM18/ha and RM75.60/day. Glyphosate 0.64 l/ha, 4 pumps/ha, 18 pumps/day; stop adding Ally. Selective spraying 438 ha (3 rounds), 100%, using mist blower at 4.6 ha/man-day, RM18/ha and RM82.80/day. Garlon 0.45 l/ha plus Ally 0.08 kg/ha, 10 pumps/day (potential 12–14); use Garlon only. Immature: 295 of 354 ha (5 of 6 rounds, 83%) for both circle and selective. Agro: 2025 weeding was completed with some delays. As of February 2026 only 1 inter-row round had been done in P13 and P16 and circle weeding had not started. Some P99 circles are overrun by Ageratum conyzoides. The over-spraying seen in P16 last time has not recurred.

MANURING: PA: 22 of 22 mature rounds (100%) in 2025, plus 5 of 5 rounds in PR24. Wheelbarrow output was 20 bags/man-day (target now 25) at RM2.20/bag, giving RM44/day. Fertiliser goes on in a semi-circle on both sides of the palm, which the PA accepted. 2025 quantities (Agro Table 3, which ties to the PA table): Mix B 177.40 t, kieserite 11.80 t, rock phosphate 45.05 t, compound 29.10 t; total 263.35 t, 100%. Agro says 2025 had no significant delays, but the PA table shows some rounds 1–2 months late in P16 and P18. As of February 2026, 14% of the annual programme was done. Outstanding: P99 Mix B round 1 (Feb), P18 Mix B round 1 (Feb) and PR24 compound round 1 (Jan), due to late fertiliser delivery; to be caught up by end of Q1. Narrow-band application was seen in P18 and previously in P13 (Sep 2025). The programme was revised because of the fertiliser price rise; the 2027 programme will come in H2 2026.

2026 PROGRAMME (Agro Appendix 1, kg/palm): P99 Mix B 2.5 in Feb and Jun. P13 Mix B 2.50 Jan, kieserite 1.0 Mar, Mix B 2.25 Apr, RP 1.75 Jun, Mix B 2.50 Aug. P16 the same but kieserite 0.75. P18 Mix B 2.25, kieserite 0.5, Mix B 2.25, RP 2.0, Mix B 2.25. PR24A compound 1.20 Jan, 1.20 Apr, RP 1.75 Jun, compound 1.50 Jul and 1.50 Oct. PR24B compound 1.00, 1.20, RP 1.75, compound 1.20, 1.50. Mix B is 45% SOA, 40% MOP, 14% kieserite, 1% borate; compound 14:13:9:2.5.

PEST AND DISEASE: rat damage census in 2025 (Feb, May, Aug, Oct). P99 3.0, 2.5, 2.0, 2.1%. P13 3.6, 3.0, 2.8, 5.8%. P16 3.8, 2.1, 2.2, 6.5%. P18 4.1, 3.2, 3.5, 7.6%. PR24 (Agro) 8.5, 7.2, 5.6, 5.0, 6.5%. Baiting: P18 2 rounds (Storm, 102 ha), P13 and P16 1 round each in November. Threshold is 10%. Ganoderma (Agro Table 7): P99 121 of 1,477 palms, P13 2, P16 4; total 127 of 19,230. Deboling has been ad hoc. The PA wants a formal census and deboling of all fallen palms. Agro P18: rats under control, no leaf-eating pests, cattle damage still unresolved.

MATURE FIELD CONDITIONS (Agro): sporadic magnesium deficiency on terraces in P13 and P16, improving. P18 shows mild potassium deficiency in places. Harvest intervals in January: P99 12–15 days, P13 and P16 12–14 days. No ramp backlog, but 5% unripe bunches on platforms in P16. Progressive pruning is good in P13 and P16, with the over-pruning in P16 improved. P99 is partly under-pruned and needs touch-up pruning. Keep two subtending fronds. Close-planted sections of P16 still need selective thinning. P18 has runt palms to poison.

CROP (PA and Agro): 2025 yield was 29.99 t/ha, 0.1% below the 30 t/ha budget and 1.6% above 2024 (29.52). By block, 2025 (2024): P99 33.60 (35.65, -5.8%); P13 36.46 (35.99, +1.3%); P16 28.75 (28.11, +2.3%); P18 27.83 (26.91, +3.4%). Agro Appendix 2: 2022 15.54 t/ha (3,185.11 t); 2023 19.82 (3,866.80 t); 2024 29.52 (4,309.21 t); 2025 29.99 (4,378.53 t). There were 24.5 harvesting rounds against 24 budgeted; interval 13 days; outturn 97%. 2 Badang units, each with 3 cutters and 1 collector. Harvesting rate, output and earnings: P99 RM44/t, 2.23 t/man-day, RM98.12/day; P13 RM30/t, 2.32 t, RM69.60; P16 RM35/t, 2.19 t, RM76.65; P18 RM50/t, 1.42 t, RM71.00. Basic wage RM65.39. Loading: 12.51 t/day at RM8/t, RM100.08/day. Target 2.5 t/man-day in P13 and P16; the earlier GMD target was 3.0. Bunch weights Dec 2024 to Dec 2025: 1999 28.6 to 28.1 kg; 2013 13.9 to 14.9; 2016 12.6 to 14.3; 2018 7.4 to 8.2. P99 produces 9 bunches a year. Agro: yields are trending up in early 2026 and prospects are good, with over 65% of palms in prime age.

ROADS, DRAINS, PRUNING (PA): 550 chains were budgeted for grading at RM10/chain; none was graded, only patched with the backhoe. Roads are satisfactory. 50 chains of drain desilting were budgeted (RM40/chain); none was done and none is needed, so the PA advises dropping it from the budget. Pruning: 584 of 584 ha (100%). Cost per palm per year: 1999 RM1.20; 2013, 2016, 2018 RM0.70.

IMMATURE PR24 (PA and Agro): about 17 months old. 5 of 5 manuring rounds done. 13 rounds of cypermethrin against Oryctes, which has reduced but is still noticeable; Agro advises monthly spear drench or carbofuran. 200 palms supplied, with more seedlings needed. Fire from neighbouring smallholdings in the first week of February 2026 hit about 6 ha. Lower fronds were scorched, spears were unaffected and no palms died. Advice: sanitation pruning, EFB mulch, watering, and day and night patrols. Ablation has started but is incomplete; at least 3 rounds are needed, the last by June 2026. Weeds persist where Mucuna bracteata is absent. No rat recurrence.

RAINFALL (Agro): 2021 2,549 mm over 118 days; 2022 2,923 mm, 125 days; 2023 2,696 mm, 101 days; 2024 2,341 mm, 125 days; 2025 2,275 mm, 120 days. Dry months in 2025: Feb 81 mm, Mar 71 mm, Aug 57 mm.
