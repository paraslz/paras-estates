REPORT EXTRACTS
LADANG PSK (Rangkai Sempurna Sdn Bhd, Prosper group). Fact pack built from two PARAS reports: Planting Advisory (PA) Report 2/2026 by Mr Boey Chee Weng, visit 29 July 2026, reviewing January to June 2026; and Agronomy Report 1/2026 by En Alison Janari (Junior Agronomist) and Chong Kuan Ming (Senior Agronomist), visit 12 February 2026. General Manager Mr Lee Chong Yew. Senior Manager per PA: Mr Chan Wah Vee (Agro report listed En Mohd Shafirun Bin Mohd Azba). Estate Manager: En Khairol Amir Bin Idris (joined company 2 May 2025, estate 1 Sep 2025).

AREA (PA 2/2026). All 1,999.83 ha planted is mature oil palm. PM05 A1-A6 380.00 ha, PM05 B1-B5 335.00, PM05 C1-C6 414.55, PM05 D1-D3 210.20 (all planted 2005), PM06 E1-E6 344.00, PM06 F1-F6 313.65 (2006), PM19H 2.43 (2019). No immature or replant. Laterite source 2.26 ha, unplanted 9.71, Asli area 4.20; total estate 2,016.00 ha. Agro 1/2026 shows total 2,023.00 ha, with 16.71 ha unplantable (unplanted 23.17 ha, 1.15%). Except for part of P19H, palms are over 20 years old and in the declining-yield stage (Agro). Much of the estate is peat and low-lying and floods often. Agro: flooding used to last up to six months; after canal desilting, water now recedes in about 60 days.

PALM CENSUS (Agro 1/2026 Table 3). PM05A 49,598 palms, 131 SPH (-4%); PM05B 38,100, 114 SPH (printed -20%, calculates to -16%); PM06E 43,469, 126 SPH (printed -8%, about -7%); PM06F 38,681, 123 SPH (printed -10%, about -9%). All completed 2025. PM05C (56,379), PM05D (28,587) and PM19H (330) are still in progress at the assumed 136 SPH. The GMD said the census was far too slow; PARAS will help with drone/GIS census and topographic mapping.

LABOUR (PA, June 2026). Harvesters 52 vs 83 required (short 31; 1:38 ha vs 1:24 ha required). By July harvesters were 66 (1:30 ha), still 17 short; 17 more were expected in August-September. Drivers/loaders 8 of 9, weeding 10 of 18, manuring 6 of 8, mandore 0 of 1, pruning 2 of 21. Total 78 vs 140 (short 62). Workers to planted ha 1:26 vs ideal 1:12. Agro (Feb) reported a shortage of 48 workers, mainly harvesters, high turnover, and 6 new TKI workers arriving 30 Jan 2026. The PA and GMD call harvester shortage and turnover the main problem, and say it has dragged on too long.

STAFF (PA). Big reshuffle: En Mohd Bukhari (Asst) in from Insan Jaya from July; Mr Supitor (Asst) out to Juasa A from July; En Mohd Afiq out to Tagar 3 from April; En Aiman (Cadet) in from Tagar 3 from April; En Mohd Taufiq promoted from Field Conductor from Nov 2025. Staff numbers adequate, but the Assistants are new and inexperienced; the EM must train them.

GENERAL CHARGES to June (PA). Actual RM951,108.22 vs budget to date RM1,453,578.00 (annual RM2,907,133). RM475.60/ha vs RM726.85/ha, underspent 35%. Management and supervision RM357,960.46; labour and amenities RM285,475.64; other RM307,672.12.

MATURE UPKEEP to June (PA). Total RM1,837,733 (RM918.94/ha) vs budget RM2,268,936 (RM1,134.57/ha), underspent 19%; annual budget RM4,537,757. Per ha actual vs budget: weeding 128.42 vs 112.98; manuring 554.00 vs 738.01; pest and disease 45.95 vs 35.38; census 1.09 vs 3.38; drains 70.15 vs 54.14; roads and bridges 57.52 vs 73.79; soil and water conservation 0 vs 59.18; boundary 0.18 vs 1.03; pruning 61.63 vs 56.68.

CIRCLE SPRAYING (PA). 3,061.60 ha (1.5 rounds) of 3,278.95 ha (1.6 rounds) = 93%. By field: A 117%, B 90%, C 91%, D 35%, E 96%, F 130%, H 50%. Circles generally clean. CKS high-volume sprayers used at 4.4 ha/manday; PA reckons 6 ha/manday is possible, and Inter Pumps have been ordered. Piece rate RM15/ha, earnings RM66/day vs basic RM65.38. Glyphosate 0.3 l/ha. Only 9 pumps/manday (potential 12); sprayers work only 4.5 hours a day. GMD: use pure glyphosate, no Ally.

SELECTIVE SPRAYING (PA). 3,384.03 ha (1.7 rounds) of 3,999.41 ha (2 rounds) = 85%. A 72%, B 103%, C 60%, D 94%, E 100%, F 89%, H 100%. Inter-rows have improved drastically, woodies are cleared, and the new EM was praised. VOPs, Stenochlaena, brackens and Giant Paspalum remain. Mist blowers at 4.5 ha/manday, RM16/ha, earnings RM72/day, Garlon 0.36 l/ha, 6 pumps/day vs potential 10. Agro (Jan 2026): circle 62% and inter-row 32% of programme to date. 2025 full year: circle 56%, inter-row 63%. Post-flood broadleaf regrowth needs selective spraying.

MANURING. PA: 2,872.90 ha of 5,039.74 ha budgeted manured to June (57%); the ACT columns of the PA block tables add up to 2,872.90 ha, but the to-June programme in those tables adds up to about 4,906 ha, not 5,039.74 ha. The delay came from late Mix B delivery and caked NK1. Wheelbarrow application at 30 bags/manday, RM2.30/bag, RM69/day. Manuring cost RM1,107,900 vs budget RM1,475,886. EFB: only 54.11 ha of 624.70 ha, because the GM diverted EFB to the Juasa A and B immature areas. EFB rate 40 t/ha, transport RM11/t, levelling RM1.50/t. Agro: 2025 Mix B only 74% done (1,647.95 of 2,191.7 t), RP 100% (524.7 t); applications were about two months late and some ran into January 2026. 2026 Mix B was 39% done by February (218.8 of 568.1 t); pending January blocks were PM05C1-6, PM05D2, PM06F4-5 and PM19H. Quality check in PM05B-4 found 26-27 bags per person, acceptable but with some lumps and narrow bands. GMD agreed to defer the final 2025 Mix B round to 2026. Agro: Mix C may be replaced by Mix B at the same rate. Agro 2026 programme (kg/palm): A Mix C 2.00, NK1 1.75, RP 1.50 (A1-A4), Mix C 2.00, NK1 1.75; B Mix C 2.00, NK1 1.75, Mix C 2.00, NK1 1.75; C and D Mix C 2.25, NK1 2.00, Mix C 2.25, NK1/NK2 2.00; E Mix C 2.00, Mix B 1.75, RP 1.50, Mix C 2.00, Mix B 1.75; F1-3/F6 2.00, 1.75, RP 1.50, 2.00, 1.75; F4-5 2.25, 2.00, RP 1.50, 2.25, 2.00; H 2.00, 2.00, RP 1.50, 2.00, 2.00. The PA's tracked programme uses lower second-half doses (see issues).

PRUNING. PA: corrective pruning completed (626 ha carried forward from 2025; table backlog 1,298.63 ha). Progressive pruning 8,041.42 of 11,998.98 ha = 67%: A 66%, B 81% (table misprints 23%), C 66%, D 60%, E 85%, F 39%, H 83%. A task force is pruning unpruned palms in flood areas at no extra cost. Rates per ha per year: 2005 RM120, 2006 RM100, 2019 RM70; corrective RM100/ha. Agro: 2025 pruning only 74% (5,928.37 of 7,999.32 ha). By January 2026 it was 32%, with nothing done in P05A, P05D or P19H; under-pruning in D3, B4 and C6 was hurting harvesting.

PESTS AND DISEASE. Agro (Dec 2025 census): rat damage 1-4%, under control, no baiting needed. Barn owl boxes 109; 50 good, 59 poor; 46% occupied (PM05D 0 of 17, PM06E 17%). PA (census Mar-Jun 2026): damage 18% overall; A and B 20%, C 14%, D 17%, E 17%, F 17%. 3 baiting rounds done; rats to be censused quarterly and baited above 10%. Ganoderma: sporadic infections, fruiting bodies and upper stem rot in P05C2 (PA).

ROADS AND DRAINS (PA). Grading and compaction 2,643 of 2,650 chains at RM19/chain (on budget); road condition very good. Lateriting 1,240 chains budgeted, none done. Desilting: 8x6x4 ft 2,178 of 2,100 chains (RM46); 12x10x8 ft 317 of 850 chains (RM51); 30x12x10 ft canal 702 of 2,000 (table says metres) at RM520. Mounding: 27,484 palms budgeted at RM8.50/palm, none done (PA). Agro says 32,054 of 34,305 palms (93%) were mounded earlier.

HARVESTING (PA). Cost RM629,068 = RM71.77/t vs budget RM81.53/t (harvest labour 47.51, transport 19.62, incentive 1.23, top-up 3.41). 10.2 rounds vs 12 budgeted; average interval 13 days per PA (should be at most 13-14 days); outturn 95%. 12 crawlers (2 cutters + 1 collector) and 3 Badang (3 + 1); 2 EV tricycles bought for trial. Harvest rates RM45-49/t budget, RM39-40/t labour, average earnings RM67-78/day vs basic RM65.39. Agro: January 2026 intervals up to 49 days in A and 50 in B; 2025 rounds averaged 18.45 (77% of target), with A at 14.17 (59%). Overripe bunches 4-5% in P05C-06 and 8-10% in P05E-6.

CROP (PA, Jan-Jun 2026). FFB 8,765.54 t vs estimate 15,573 t (annual budget 33,146 t). Same period 2025: 10,903.58 t. Yield 4.38 t/ha vs budget 7.79 t/ha (-44%) and 5.45 t/ha last year (-20%). CPO 1,386.39 t, OER 15.82% (budget 18.51%); KER 5.00%. PA's last-year '12 months budget' column shows 33,785 t / 16.89 t/ha; this is a budget, and the Agro 2025 actual was 25,434.05 t / 12.72 t/ha. Bunch weights per PA (June 2025 to June 2026): A 13.9 to 12.8, B 13.5 to 11.6, C 18.0 to 20.1, D 18.2 to 19.2, E 13.6 to 13.2, F 14.5 to 13.1, P19H 2.6 to 8.0 kg. Causes of the shortfall: harvester shortage, low-crop season and flooded peat. Agro yields (full years): 2024 12.89 and 2025 12.72 t/ha; by field 2025 A 7.85, B 11.80, C 18.22, D 14.59, E 11.52, F 12.33, H 19.59. January 2026 only: 0.85 t/ha vs 0.83 in January 2025 (+2%). Block January yields (Agro Appendix 3(b), t/ha, Jan 2026 vs Jan 2025): A 0.23 vs 0.75, B 0.37 vs 0.13 (Table 17 prints 0.30), C 1.30 vs 1.18, D 1.29 vs 1.15, E 0.97 vs 0.76 (Table 17 prints 0.79), F 1.11 vs 1.08, H 1.28 vs 0.82.

COST OF PRODUCTION (PA, to June). RM389.93 per t FFB vs estimate RM320.56 (annual budget RM301.22): general charges 108.51 vs 93.34; upkeep 209.65 vs 145.70; harvesting 71.77 vs 81.53. Per t CPO RM2,465.33 vs RM1,731.58. Cost is high because crop is below budget. Capex RM16,358 vs budget RM17,110 (computers, staff quarters furniture, fibre boat).

RAINFALL (Agro Appendix 2). 2023: 4,215.5 mm (stated 180 rain days; monthly rows add to 204). 2024: 3,770.5 mm, 136 days. 2025: 3,637.5 mm, 131 days. January 2026: 101 mm in 6 days.

ADVISORS' BOTTOM LINE. PA: recruit harvesters and sprayers, switch to Inter Pumps, raise pumps per manday and hours worked, clear the pruning backlog, start mounding, train the new Assistants, and fix prolonged flooding. Agro: keep strictly to the fertiliser programme, since deviations over two years have depressed yield and bunch weight; shorten harvest intervals; speed up census; maintain barn owl boxes.
