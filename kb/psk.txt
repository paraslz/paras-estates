AREA (PA 2/2026). All 1,999.83 ha planted is mature oil palm. PM05 A1-A6 380.00 ha, PM05 B1-B5 335.00, PM05 C1-C6 414.55, PM05 D1-D3 210.20 (all planted 2005), PM06 E1-E6 344.00, PM06 F1-F6 313.65 (2006), PM19H 2.43 (2019). No immature or replant.
